A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.6.0-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.5.11-beta

**The first of Guerrilla's activities.** Work on Guerrilla's activities has started, with the demolitions master challenges:
- Stand at a challenge's spot (the first is by the bar about 360 m north-west of Dust's square) and open the console: it names the challenge there. Start it with `rfg_dm_start je_dm_01`.
- Guerrilla's rules: bring the target down before the timer runs out without leaving the area, for salvage and a best time. After any activity, Guerrilla's cooldown (3 minutes) applies.
- `rfg_district` shows the district you're in, its EDF control and morale; `rfg_district_set control|morale N` sets them, to try things out.

**The EDF see you as Guerrilla's do.**
- **Undercover:** at a green alert the EDF take you for a civilian until you attack them, they see you in one of their bases, or the alert rises.
- **Restricted areas:** Guerrilla's 40 EDF bases. The EDF who see you inside one take you for an enemy, and the alert won't fall while you're there.

**Death pits and minefields.** Guerrilla's 21 pits and 4 minefields kill whoever goes in, as in Guerrilla.

**All ten backpacks.** Buy and wear them in **F7 → Guerrilla → Upgrades → Backpacks**, and switch one on with **B**:
- each sits on Mason's back, with its fuel gauge (cyan with enough fuel, red without, yellow while on) and its own effects, camera shakes and sounds;
- Thrust throws you up, with a slam-down if you hold jump; Rhino charges through people, vehicles and walls; Stealth fades you out and the EDF lose you while you're faded; Concussion throws everyone near you; Tremor shakes the ground; Firepower's bullets explode where they land; Vision shows people through walls; Heal, Fleetfoot and the jetpack as in Guerrilla.

**Mason's coat** swings as he moves and blows in the wind.

**Wind turbines** turn, each at its own angle. Shoot the blades and the motor sparks, blows and drops them; knock a tower down and its blades come down with it. The mining machine's blade turns and the cranes' hooks swing.

**The HUD:**
- **Guerrilla Handbook:** the first item in the pause menu, with Guerrilla's 90 entries on 7 pages. Entries open as you go (weapons reaching your locker, radio tags found); new ones are marked.
- **Tool tips:** Guerrilla's help boxes pop up as things first happen (a weapon locker opened, salvage picked up, a car got into, the alert raised), as often as Guerrilla shows them. `ToolTips=0` under `[GuerrillaHud]` turns them off.
- **Messages:** Guerrilla's pickup rows, messages and use prompts.
- **The minimap's radar sweep:** Guerrilla's scan line runs down the minimap; people light up as it passes and fade behind it. The EDF show only while they fight you, unless you've bought the Personnel Detector.
- **Change Guerrilla's keys** in **F7 → Guerrilla → Keys** (the map, the backpack, the map's zoom, the shop's lists, closing a tool tip). They're saved under `[GuerrillaKeys]`.

**Guns and ammo:**
- A gun dropped by the EDF gives Guerrilla's rounds, and each gun carries Guerrilla's ammo (more once you buy its ammo upgrade).
- An ammo crate refills you once, until it streams in again; a weapon locker every time, as in Guerrilla.

**Moving and fighting:**
- Standing still, the camera turns freely: Mason keeps his heading and looks ahead instead of twisting round to face it. Aiming, attacking and moving are as before. Darius too.
- A standing hammer swing holds Mason in place, as Guerrilla's does.

**People:** civilians walk about the towns. People far away no longer sink into slopes or fall out of the world.

**The level editor** has a new look and layout: Fluent controls, panels you can dock, tab and float, and an asset browser. If it stops answering, it writes what it was doing to its log.

**Fixed:**
- Bringing a building down could drop the frame rate sharply for a second or two.
- Escape in the weapon locker opened the pause menu as well.
- Armageddon's "[E] Enter" prompt showed under Guerrilla's, and an empty grey box at the top right.
- Opening Options → Controls in the Guerrilla world ran the interface's memory out ("dlmalloc out of memory").
- Red "Invalid transition navpoint", "Invalid ladder or hole transition navpoint" and "Failed pathfind" errors around blown-open buildings and in Eos.
- "Ran out of slots for object type (automobile)": the world keeps Guerrilla's limit of cars, removing the farthest empty one out of sight as Guerrilla does.
- Buildings far away dropped out and came back far more often while you drove or flew (`StreamSteady=` under `[GuerrillaWorld]`). A long pause in loading while driving fast through new ground can still leave it blurry for a moment.

Everything from v0.5.11-beta is here too: Guerrilla's sounds, the alert level, guns on vehicles, saving in the Guerrilla world, the map, the safehouse upgrade shops, Guerrilla's people, every weapon, the vehicles.

**Not there yet:**
- **Activities:** only the demolitions master, and only from the console: no prompt at its spot, no briefing, timer or results screens yet, your own weapons instead of the challenge's, and the target stays down after a win. The other activities come next.
- **Control and morale:** the numbers are there (`rfg_district`), but nothing in the world changes them yet. The EDF's reinforcements, Red Faction fighters joining your fights and civilians taking up arms come after the activities.
- **Stealth** may look more solid under its shimmer than in Guerrilla.
- **Looks:** some of Guerrilla's materials and shaders are still Armageddon's.
- **Sounds:** no music or voices of Guerrilla's yet.
- **Dying:** Guerrilla's respawn at a safehouse isn't ported yet. At the death screen, press **~** and then **R** to reload the last checkpoint.
- **Story:** Guerrilla's missions and story come last of all.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from any earlier version:** your world has to be built again (the launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No). Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 3 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (a few minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu: the Handbook and SAVE GAME are there.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.5.11-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds the new settings to your file and keeps your values, among them a new `[GuerrillaKeys]` section (your old `MapKey=` still works). Your file keeps `Activities=0` under `[GuerrillaCampaign]`: set it to `1` to try the demolitions master. Then build the world again, as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
