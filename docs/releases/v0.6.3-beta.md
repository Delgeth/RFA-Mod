A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.6.3-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.6.2-beta

**The districts come alive.**
- Destroying EDF buildings lowers the district's EDF control, by Guerrilla's points. Killing civilians or guerrillas, or the EDF killing them near you, lowers morale; wrecking EDF propaganda raises it; dying costs some.
- Guerrilla's control and morale meters slide as they change, with the district's name.
- `rfg_district` in the console shows the district you're in; `rfg_liberated` shows Guerrilla's district-liberated screen.
- All of it is saved inside each of your saves.

**People join your fights.**
- During a fight, civilians near you take up arms and join the Red Faction. How many depends on the district's morale: none below 20 %, up to 12 at 100 %.
- At orange or red alert, Red Faction squads run in from the roads to back you up, bigger and sooner the higher the morale.
- Both go home when the alert drops to green.

**Marauder ambushes.** Guerrilla's 61 ambush areas: walk into one and Marauders may jump you.

**Civilians react.** They run from gunfire and explosions, cower when cornered or when a car comes at them, stop to gawk at tanks and walkers, and curl up on the ground for a while after a beating.

**Respawning.** When you die, the death screen offers **CONTINUE**: you wake at the nearest safehouse in that district, alive, with the weapons and ammo you had, and the world as you left it.

**Demolitions master.** Walk up to a challenge's start point and press use: Guerrilla's briefing, the timer panel, the area ring on the minimap, the 5-second warning when you leave, the results, and the warp back to the start. The target building is rebuilt for a replay.

**Story people at the safehouses:** Samanya, OldCoot and the Red Faction commander nearby, a VIP (Carmen, Jorge or Kyle) at most safehouse visits, and the radio operator in Eos. For now they stand at their spots.

**Also:**
- 64 cars at once, twice Guerrilla's 32, so there's more traffic.
- The Mohole in the Badlands is the deep pit it should be, and a few other deep pits are open again (with their death triggers).
- The minimap's icons are Guerrilla's size, and the safehouse and mission icons sit on the frame's edge.
- The ammo counter uses Guerrilla's own number font and no longer keeps flashing after a reload.
- The Gutter can be used as Mason's melee weapon.
- The kiosks' newsreader takes a third of the disk space.
- **F7 → Limits** has rows for cars, flyers, walkers, turrets and people slots.

Everything from v0.6.2-beta is here too: the kiosks, shooting riders, taking cars, changing seats, the minimap's icons and zoom, all ten backpacks, the Handbook, tool tips, undercover and restricted areas, the death pits, Mason's coat and the wind turbines.

**Known problems:**
- **The game may crash when you fly very fast in slew mode** (the debug camera) far from Mason. A likely cause is fixed in this release, but it isn't tested yet.
- **An invisible vehicle** at the Dust EDF base can throw you across the map if you touch it.
- **Civilians walk around with their rifles out**, so they look like guerrillas, and **Marauders can throw grenades without pause**, knocking you down until you die.
- **Demolitions master:** some challenges fail as soon as they start (their area is centred in the wrong place), the challenge's own weapons and supplies (barrels, propane) aren't there yet, and you keep your own guns.
- **The story people may not show** where they should.
- **Kiosk broadcasts play at full volume from anywhere nearby, and sounds stop at the edge of their range instead of fading.**
- The prompt for swapping a weapon on the ground overlaps the weapon wheel, and the count of remote charges you've placed isn't shown.
- Some of Guerrilla's materials and shaders are still Armageddon's; no music or NPC voices yet.
- Guerrilla's missions and story come last of all.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from any earlier version:** your world has to be built again (the launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No). Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 3 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (a few minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu: the Handbook and SAVE GAME are there.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.6.2-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds the new settings to your file and keeps your values (`Converts=` and `GuerrillaSquads=` under `[GuerrillaCampaign]`, among others). Your file keeps `Activities=` as you had it: set it to `1` for the demolitions master. Then build the world again, as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
