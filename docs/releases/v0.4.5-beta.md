A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.4.5-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.4.4-beta

**Nothing of Armageddon's levels is left in the Guerrilla world.** v0.4.4 wrote the terrain, zones and buildings from scratch; the last pieces still taken from Armageddon's own levels are now Guerrilla's own too:

- **The rocks** (Guerrilla's caps, covers and clumps of rock on the slopes) are converted the way the buildings are: their own mesh, materials and collision shape, instead of being fitted into one of Armageddon's boulders.
- **The AI's navigation.** Each of Guerrilla's 41 playable zones carries its own navigation, the paths its people walk and its vehicles drive, in place of the Ruin arena's grid moved to every zone. The scenery zones around the map have none, as in Guerrilla. Nothing uses it yet (there are no people yet), but it is ready for them.
- **No buried floor.** Every zone had a flat floor 150 m under the ground, from Armageddon's levels. Guerrilla has none, and now neither does the world.
- **The time of day.** Each district's light, sky, fog and colour all come from Guerrilla's own settings for that district. Before, they were laid over Armageddon's own settings, and a few of those were left over:
  - the far mountains around the map (the backdrop) were drawn as one flat haze colour; they now show their own picture, faint in the haze;
  - smoke and dust are lit by the district's light, as in Guerrilla, rather than plain white;
  - the glow around bright light follows Guerrilla's settings.

  The sky, the sun, the shading and the fog are as before.
- **The roads' material** is written by the world builder too. The roads look as before.

**Not there yet:** people, vehicles and plants; Guerrilla's missions and story; its far mountains up close (from high up the far ground looks smooth and smeared); effects when props break; the minimap. The sky isn't fogged yet: Guerrilla fades its sky into the haze near the horizon. Glass and effects aren't fogged. Some tall structures can stay hanging in the air while a thin support still holds them. About 2,000 objects use settings Armageddon's tables don't have, so they don't show on the map or smoke when destroyed yet.

## Rebuilding the Guerrilla world

Build the world again to get the new rocks, navigation and time of day:

1. Run `editor\GuerrillaWorld.exe` again (two or three minutes).
2. In the game, open the console with the ` key and type `level rfg_12_11` (or any zone's name).

A world built with an older version still opens, as it was.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 1 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. In `rfa_mods.ini`, set `GuerrillaDir=` under `[Launcher]` (just below `GameDir=`) to Guerrilla's folder, for example `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Red Faction Guerrilla`. Both tools read it. (Or give the folder to each tool as its argument.)
3. Run `editor\GuerrillaWorld.exe` (two or three minutes).
4. Run `editor\GuerrillaHud.exe` for Guerrilla's HUD (a few seconds). `editor\GuerrillaHud.exe --remove` takes it away again; remove it only that way, since one of its files also holds the pictures of Armageddon's own HUD.
5. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.

Run the tools again after updating RFA-Mod. To take the world away, remove the HUD first (`--remove`), then delete `rfg_*`, `zonescript_rfg.vpp_pc`, `rfg.gtodx`, `table.vpp_pc\territories.xtbl` and `misc.vpp_pc\bitmaps_pc` from `overrides\build\pc\cache\`.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and its tools: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.4.4-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini` (there are no new settings). Then rebuild the Guerrilla world as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
