A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.6.5-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.6.4-beta

**A new main menu, thanks to Delgeth.**
- The main menu is grouped by game, Red Faction: Armageddon and Red Faction: Guerrilla, with New Game, New Game+, Load Game and the DLC under Campaign.
- **Options → Main Menu** sets its layout and what it shows, a **Modern** style in an accent colour of your choice, and any of the game's music to play behind it.
- **Level editor → Create level** asks Ruin or Infestation and opens the level editor's New level for it; **Delete custom level** takes one away. If a level fails to load, the game goes back to the main menu and says so.

**The level editor, also thanks to Delgeth:** a navigation mesh painter (N), and road, spline and logic editors.

**Liberating districts.**
- A district is liberated, as in Guerrilla, when the EDF's control there reaches zero and its story missions are done. The missions aren't ported yet, so to try it type `rfg_district_missions_done` in the console, then bring the EDF's control down (or `rfg_district_set control 0`).
- Guerrilla's district-liberated screen shows, and the district's upgrades go on sale in the safehouse shop.
- As the campaign goes on, newly made squads carry better weapons: the Red Faction's with your tech level, the EDF's and the Marauders' as districts fall.
- Guerrilla's combat values in the Guerrilla world: enemy damage, aim, bursts, your health's regeneration, vehicle damage and more.
- With a district's morale at 30 % or more, sprint on foot for 10 seconds at green alert and a guerrilla brings you a vehicle.

**The EDF answer the alert.**
- At yellow and orange, the EDF send their nearest squad after you. At orange and red they send reinforcements on foot, in vehicles and by air, from their own squad spots and the roads. Climb high and they send flyers.
- At orange and red, civilians clear off out of your sight, and traffic thins out.
- The EDF's drive-bys, fly-bys and patrols only start at green alert, away from safehouses, when no activity is running and not in a liberated district.

**Demolitions master's supplies.** The challenges now bring their own propane tanks, explosive barrels, ammo boxes, walkers and rocket turret, as in Guerrilla, and take them away when the challenge ends.

**Voices and sound.**
- Guerrilla's people talk: 89 voices and 5,362 lines, for pain, dying, spotting you, grenades, panic, joining a fight, ambushes and greetings.
- Every 3D sound now fades with distance the way Guerrilla's does, kiosk broadcasts included.
- Fixed: all sound cutting out in the Guerrilla world.

**Life in the world.**
- Guards hold Guerrilla's guard posts and fight from them.
- People at their spots use their props: cigarettes, Samanya's welding torch, binoculars and more.
- Marauders ambush you from their vehicles, and skirmish among themselves.
- Walkers drink, tire and work, by the hour, the district's morale and where they are.
- Guerrilla's 319 pieces of pre-made damage on its buildings.

**Driving:** Guerrilla's steering force (it was missing), its tyre grip, and its rules for how a wheel finds the ground.

**Also:**
- Fixed freezes: an object falling into the bottom of the world, a car's route going round in a loop, and the short pauses while the log was written.
- Fixed crashes: getting into a car with many dented cars around, a vehicle taken away while a save loads, quitting Ruin from its pause menu then loading another level, and the error when quitting the game with the MCP servers on.
- During a demolitions master challenge, Mason keeps the challenge's weapons instead of getting his hammer back.
- A quieter log: each mod's routine lines only with its own `Log=1` in `rfa_mods.ini`.
- For developers: the MCP servers (the editor's and the live game's) are off by default. Turn them on with `Enabled=1` under `[Mcp]` in `rfa_mods.ini`.

Everything from v0.6.4-beta is here too: life in the towns, the editor's view of the Guerrilla world's gameplay, the districts' control and morale, respawning at safehouses, people joining your fights, the kiosks and the minimap.

**Known problems:**
- **Cars stop dead at walls**, and at many signs and poles, instead of breaking through: a hit damages the pieces, but the links between them don't give way yet.
- **Steering is still rough at speed:** a wheel loses the ground over small dips.
- **Guerrilla's walkers walk on the spot** when you drive one.
- **Mason can't take cover** against walls yet.
- The demolitions master's supplies, the vehicle drop-off and the EDF's reinforcements at orange and red alert are new and haven't had much testing in game yet.
- **Mission briefing terminals** do nothing yet, and one demolitions master challenge's chemical tanks don't go off: both wait for Guerrilla's missions and scripts, which come last of all.
- If the game freezes, wait a few seconds before closing it: the log then records where it was stuck. Please send `logs\rfa_mods.log`.
- Some of Guerrilla's materials and shaders are still Armageddon's, and there's no music yet.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from any earlier version:** your world has to be built again (the launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No). Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 3 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (a few minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu: the Handbook and SAVE GAME are there.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.6.4-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds any new settings to your file and keeps your values. Then build the world again, as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
