A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.5.10-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.5.9-beta

**Saving in the Guerrilla world.** The pause menu in the Guerrilla world now has **SAVE GAME**: it saves the moment you're in, then opens the save screen for you to pick a slot. **LOAD GAME** brings you back into the Guerrilla world where you saved.
- A save there only goes into the slot you pick. It never writes into a slot on its own, so your campaign's autosave stays as it was.
- **LOAD CHECKPOINT** goes back to your last save.
- It's a new mod, `guerrilla_save.dll`; `Worlds=` under the new `[GuerrillaSave]` section names the worlds it saves in.

Everything from v0.5.9-beta is here too: Guerrilla's map icons and world map, the safehouse upgrade shops, radio tags, Guerrilla's people, every weapon, the vehicles.

**Not there yet:**
- **Saving:** your upgrades and the radio tags you've found are one set for all your saves (they're kept between games, but loading an older save doesn't take them back). There's no autosave yet, and after a load you start where you saved, not at a safehouse as in Guerrilla.
- **Weapon cabinets:** the safehouses' weapon cabinets don't open yet, and a red marker shows in front of them.
- **The map:** no route to a waypoint yet (Guerrilla's GPS line), and no player markers. The M.O.A.B. itself (the car bomb) isn't ported yet, only its place on the map.
- **The HUD:** Guerrilla's alert level and its messages aren't there yet.
- **Turrets:** on Guerrilla's vehicles with a turret (the Kitsune, and likely the APC and staff car), the gun doesn't show on the roof yet.
- **People:** they mostly stand where they appear. Guerrilla's ambient people (civilians walking about, Marauder ambushes) aren't ported yet.
- **Dying:** Guerrilla's respawn at a safehouse isn't ported yet. At the death screen, press **~** and then **R** to reload the last checkpoint. After that reload the people may be gone and vehicles from **F7 → Spawn** may not appear; for a full world, load a save or start the game again.
- **Story:** Guerrilla's missions and story come last of all.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from v0.5.9-beta:** nothing to build again.

**Coming from v0.5.8-beta:** build the HUD again. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`, tick **HUD** and click **Build** (a minute or so). Your world stays as it is.

**Coming from v0.5.7-beta or older:** your world has to be built again (the launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No). Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 2 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (two or three minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu's SAVE GAME.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.5.9-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds the new settings to your file and keeps your values: `guerrilla_save=` under `[Mods]`, and a new `[GuerrillaSave]` section with `Worlds=`.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
