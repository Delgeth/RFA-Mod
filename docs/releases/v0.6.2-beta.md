A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.6.2-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.6.1-beta

**The EDF's news kiosks.** Guerrilla's kiosks work in the Guerrilla world, through a new mod, `guerrilla_kiosks.dll`:
- Their screens show the EDF news pictures, switch to the alert picture above green, and go dark when you shoot a screen or blow the kiosk up.
- At a green alert, stand near one and it plays a broadcast: a jingle and music, a few lines from the EDF newsreader with the screens changing picture between them, then the sign-off. Walk away and it stops; the next one comes a couple of minutes later. The radio in a bar plays them too.

**Vehicles:**
- **Shoot the people in them:** gunners and riders can be shot and killed in their seats, as in Guerrilla (Armageddon made anyone in a vehicle bulletproof). You're still safe from bullets in your own vehicle.
- **Take a car from its driver:** walk right up to a civilian's or guerrilla's car (within 2 m) and press **E**: the driver gets out and you get in. As in Guerrilla, a vehicle with a live EDF soldier in it can't be taken; deal with him first.
- **Change seats:** press the **middle mouse button** to move from the driver's seat to the vehicle's gun and aim it yourself, and again to go back.

**The minimap** draws more of Guerrilla's icons, by Guerrilla's rules:
- the nearest safehouse, and mission starts nearby, pinned to the edge;
- activity starts (by alert level), grey once you've done them; Guerrilla Express starts;
- the EDF's important buildings as targets;
- your placed remote charges, mines and singularity bombs;
- the north marker, and rings on the radio tags you haven't found.

**Page Up / Page Down** zoom the minimap, as Guerrilla's ZOOM IN / ZOOM OUT MINIMAP did (change them in **F7 → Guerrilla → Keys**).

**Also:**
- **F7 → Guerrilla → Keys** now takes the new key when you press it.
- The ammo counter uses Guerrilla's own number font, and its left number collapses while you reload.
- The backpacks, ammo box, propane tank, weapon locker and riot shield, and the guns' low-detail copies, now have Guerrilla's full texture sizes.
- More of the HUD's text is at Guerrilla's size.

Everything from v0.6.1-beta is here too: the editor listing the Guerrilla world's files, the first activity (demolitions master), all ten backpacks, the Handbook, tool tips, undercover and restricted areas, the death pits, Mason's coat and the wind turbines.

**Not there yet / known problems:**
- **The ammo counter** can keep flashing for a while after a reload has finished.
- **Kiosks:** the broadcast's news lines don't unlock as you win activities yet, and the people who stand and watch a kiosk in Guerrilla aren't ported.
- **Activities:** only the demolitions master, from the console (`Activities=1` under `[GuerrillaCampaign]`; see the v0.6.0-beta notes).
- **Looks:** some of Guerrilla's materials and shaders are still Armageddon's.
- **Dying:** Guerrilla's respawn at a safehouse isn't ported yet. At the death screen, press **~** and then **R** to reload the last checkpoint.
- **Story:** Guerrilla's missions and story come last of all.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from any earlier version:** your world has to be built again (the launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No). Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked. The kiosks' newsreader adds about 110 MB to the world.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 3 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (a few minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu: the Handbook and SAVE GAME are there.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.6.1-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds the new settings to your file and keeps your values: `guerrilla_kiosks=` under `[Mods]`, a new `[GuerrillaKiosks]` section, `RiderDamageAsGuerrilla=` and `ChangeSeatsAsGuerrilla=` under `[GuerrillaVehicles]`, and `MinimapZoomIn=` / `MinimapZoomOut=` under `[GuerrillaKeys]`. Then build the world again, as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
