A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.6.6-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.6.5-beta

**Sound fades with distance, for real this time.**
- Kiosk broadcasts, the bars' radios, fires and other placed sounds now fade out as you walk away, the way Guerrilla's do. Before, they stayed near full volume and then cut off at the edge of their range: every fade was written in the wrong units and played at about a tenth of its strength.
- Engines blend their sound layers as the revs and the load change, at Guerrilla's levels.
- Fixed: sound breaking up and dropping out. Guerrilla's sounds are now compressed, so they stream in a third of the space, and the sound engine has more room to play them.

**The world looks more like Guerrilla's.**
- **Sun shafts** now show when the sun is behind a ridge or a building, and when it's just off the screen, as in Re-Mars-tered.
- **Wheel dust:** cars kick up dust and dirt from their wheels.
- **Guerrilla's 475 m view distance:** zones and buildings finish loading out of sight, in the haze, instead of appearing in front of you. To see farther, raise **F7 → Graphics → View distance** (up to 1,500 m), or `FarClip=` under `[GuerrillaWorld]` in `rfa_mods.ini`.
- **Guerrilla's placed effects:** smoke, steam, sparks, force fields and the Mohole's effects, where Guerrilla puts them. Smokestacks smoke, vents and antennas, PA speakers, fire barrels, power cores and mission computers work as in Guerrilla.
- **Gusts of wind** blow dust off the ground.

**Driving:** the ground the cars drive on is now Guerrilla's own, with its heights and the way its triangles are split, so wheels follow it as they do in Guerrilla.

**Barrels and propane tanks.**
- Barrels and other loose props roll and come to rest on their sides, instead of flipping onto their lids and staying there.
- The sledgehammer knocks them about, as in Guerrilla, instead of destroying them with one swing.
- A thrown propane tank bounces off what it hits. Shoot it to set it off.

**Activities.**
- Activities show their start icons on the map where Guerrilla shows them.
- Guerrilla's activity-complete screen, with your results, at the end of a Transporter or a Collateral Damage.

Everything from v0.6.5-beta is here too: liberating districts, the EDF's answer to the alert, the demolitions master's supplies, Guerrilla's people's voices, and Delgeth's main menu and level editor tools.

**Known problems:**
- **Picking up a propane tank for the first time** can put it in your weapon select, with a large shape stuck to Mason's hands. Change weapons, then pick the tank up again.
- **Cars stop dead at walls**, and at many signs and poles, instead of breaking through: a hit damages the pieces, but the links between them don't give way yet.
- **Steering may still be rough at speed**: the new ground hasn't had much driving yet.
- **Guerrilla's walkers walk on the spot** when you drive one.
- **Mason can't take cover** against walls yet.
- The wheel dust, placed effects, gusts, map icons and activity-complete screen are new and haven't had much testing in game yet. The same goes for the demolitions master's supplies, the vehicle drop-off and the EDF's reinforcements at orange and red alert.
- **Mission briefing terminals** do nothing yet, and one demolitions master challenge's chemical tanks don't go off: both wait for Guerrilla's missions and scripts, which come last of all.
- If the game freezes, wait a few seconds before closing it: the log then records where it was stuck. Please send `logs\rfa_mods.log`.
- Some of Guerrilla's materials and shaders are still Armageddon's, and there's no music yet.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from any earlier version:** your world has to be built again (the launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No). Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 3 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (a few minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu: the Handbook and SAVE GAME are there.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.6.5-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds any new settings to your file and keeps your values. Then build the world again, as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
