A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.5.0-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.4.9-beta

**Guerrilla's vehicles.** A new tool, `editor\GuerrillaArsenal.exe`, builds Guerrilla's vehicles from your own copy of Guerrilla: 62 models, from cars, trucks, buses, ATVs, rovers, EDF staff cars, scouts and APCs, Marauder raiders, jetters and flyers. Spawn one with `drop_car` in the console (`drop_car Taxi`, `ATV`, `Bluecrab`, `Staffcar`, `Caravan`, `MiningRover`, `Raider`, ...; the tool lists every name when it's done) and drive it with Guerrilla's keys: W accelerates, S brakes and reverses, Space is the handbrake, A and D steer.

**Getting in and out the way Mason does.** Darius uses Guerrilla's own animations for vehicles, moved over from Mason: opening the door and climbing in, quick, diving and side exits, and moving between seats. E gets you out and the middle mouse button moves you to another seat, keys Armageddon never gave the PC. E also gets you out of Armageddon's own vehicles where the game lets you. The keys are `Exit=` and `ChangeSeat=` under `[GuerrillaVehicles]` in `rfa_mods.ini`.

**Loose props fly when you hit them.** Barrels and other loose props in the Guerrilla world now go flying when you hit them with the sledgehammer, as in Guerrilla, instead of breaking apart or swinging in place like a punching bag.

**Guerrilla's explosions.** Explosive barrels go up in Guerrilla's own fireball and shards, and chemical tanks with Guerrilla's explosion. The game holds only 132 kinds of explosion and has room for 2 more, so the other 12 that Guerrilla's props use wait for now: those props blow up with Armageddon's explosion of the same name where it has one.

**Fewer files in `mods\`.** Borderless, FOV, video settings and the console are now one file, `mods\rfa_qol.dll`. Each still has its own switch under `[Mods]` and its own settings, so nothing changes in your `rfa_mods.ini`.

**Not there yet:** people and plants; Guerrilla's missions and story; its far mountains up close; the minimap; effects' sounds, the gusts of dust along the ground and the dust devils, the dust thinning right beside a building, and the effects placed on their own around the map. Glass and effects aren't fogged. Some tall structures can stay hanging in the air while a thin support still holds them.

For the vehicles: no sounds, lights or turrets yet (the doors open silently), and their glass shows too bright. Darius is a little taller than Mason, so his hands may not land exactly on the wheel and door. Tanks and the bulldozer aren't built yet, and walkers can't be got into yet. `drop_car` may leave a vehicle waiting to load: the game only loads a vehicle a level has set memory aside for, which the Guerrilla world doesn't do yet.

## The Guerrilla world

**Build your world again** with this version's `editor\GuerrillaWorld.exe`: the props' physics and explosions are made when the world is built. Then run `editor\GuerrillaArsenal.exe` for the vehicles.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 1 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. In `rfa_mods.ini`, set `GuerrillaDir=` under `[Launcher]` (just below `GameDir=`) to Guerrilla's folder, for example `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Red Faction Guerrilla`. All three tools read it. (Or give the folder to each tool as its argument.)
3. Run `editor\GuerrillaWorld.exe` (a few minutes).
4. Run `editor\GuerrillaHud.exe` for Guerrilla's HUD (a few seconds). `editor\GuerrillaHud.exe --remove` takes it away again; remove it only that way, since one of its files also holds the pictures of Armageddon's own HUD.
5. Run `editor\GuerrillaArsenal.exe` for Guerrilla's vehicles (a few seconds). `--vehicles Col_Taxi_1,Min_ATV_1` builds only some; `--remove` takes them away again.
6. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
7. To drive, type `drop_car Taxi` in the console, walk up to its driver side and get in with E.

Run the tools again after updating RFA-Mod. To take the world away, remove the HUD and the vehicles first (each tool's `--remove`), then delete `rfg_*`, `zonescript_rfg.vpp_pc`, `rfg.gtodx`, `table.vpp_pc\territories.xtbl`, `effects.vpp_pc\vfx_containers.asm_pc` and `misc.vpp_pc\bitmaps_pc` from `overrides\build\pc\cache\`.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and its tools: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.4.9-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`; the launcher adds this version's new settings (`Exit=` and `ChangeSeat=`) to your file. You can delete the old `borderless.dll`, `console.dll`, `fov.dll` and `video_settings.dll` from `mods\`. If you leave them, they're skipped, and your settings for them work as before.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
