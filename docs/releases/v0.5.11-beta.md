A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.5.11-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.5.10-beta

**Guerrilla's sounds.** The Guerrilla world sounds like Guerrilla now, where before most of it was silent or Armageddon's:
- **Vehicles:** every vehicle's engine, with the full sound for the one you drive and the distant one for the others; tyres on gravel; horns; crashes and scrapes.
- **Guns:** each gun's fire with Guerrilla's start, loop and tail, its far version when the shot is a long way off, the click of an empty gun and its reload.
- **Hits and explosions:** bullets and the hammer on each material, the explosions with their far versions.
- **Destruction:** pieces breaking off, falling, sliding and groaning as a building comes down.
- **The world's own sounds:** fires, vents, the EDF's screens.

**The alert level.** Guerrilla's alert level runs in the Guerrilla world, with Guerrilla's own numbers: it rises while the EDF sees you, when you kill its people, wreck its vehicles or damage its buildings, and falls again once you get away. The light by the minimap shows it: green, yellow, orange, red. It's a new mod, `guerrilla_campaign.dll`. The EDF's answer to a high alert (reinforcements) isn't ported yet.

**Guns on vehicles.** The roof guns of Guerrilla's vehicles are drawn and work.
- While you drive, the gun is yours as in Guerrilla: it points straight ahead, turns onto an enemy that is nearly ahead of you, and your fire button fires it. To aim it yourself, change to the gunner's seat.
- The EDF's gunners use theirs.
- `DriverGunAsGuerrilla=0` under `[GuerrillaVehicles]` makes the driver's gun follow your aim point instead.

**Throwing.**
- Satchel charges are thrown by hand, and R sets them off.
- Pick up a propane tank and throw it: it goes off where it lands.

**Ammo boxes.** Walk up to one of the EDF's ammo boxes and Guerrilla's prompt appears; E fills your guns.

**The minimap** shows dots for the people and vehicles near you.

**The level editor** (by Delgeth):
- It edits the Guerrilla world. Its changes go into a new `edits\` folder, so the world you built stays as the builder made it.
- **Sculpt** (T) shapes a zone's ground: raise, lower, smooth, flatten. What stands on the ground moves with it.
- It works with an Xbox controller: fly around, grab, turn, copy and delete objects, and sculpt.

`docs\editor-guide.md` has the details.

**Fixed:**
- All sound could stop in a big fight, and engines could go silent for good. The game's limits on sounds were made for Armageddon's own; they are raised for Guerrilla's.
- People, vehicle parts and plants could blink out in a busy place, with "Out of unbatched renderable trackers" in red at the top of the screen. That list holds as many as Guerrilla's now.
- The propane tanks and the safehouses' weapon lockers were bright red.
- Guards who couldn't find a way stood still with an error; they hold their post now.
- `build.cmd` works from a folder with an apostrophe in its path (for those who build from source).

Everything from v0.5.10-beta is here too: saving in the Guerrilla world, the map, the safehouse upgrade shops, Guerrilla's people, every weapon, the vehicles.

**Not there yet:**
- **Sounds:** no music, voices or footsteps of Guerrilla's yet (footsteps are Armageddon's). The machine-gun turrets turn silently. Sounds aren't muffled by distance or by walls yet.
- **Guns on vehicles:** a hard turn, a skid or a bump can still throw the driver's gun off its aim for a moment.
- **The alert level:** it shows and counts, but the EDF doesn't send reinforcements for it yet.
- **Saving:** your upgrades and the radio tags you've found are one set for all your saves (they're kept between games, but loading an older save doesn't take them back). There's no autosave yet, and after a load you start where you saved, not at a safehouse as in Guerrilla.
- **Weapon lockers:** the safehouses' weapon lockers don't open yet.
- **The map:** no route to a waypoint yet (Guerrilla's GPS line), and no player markers. The M.O.A.B. itself (the car bomb) isn't ported yet, only its place on the map.
- **The HUD:** Guerrilla's messages aren't there yet.
- **People:** they mostly stand where they appear. Guerrilla's ambient people (civilians walking about, Marauder ambushes) aren't ported yet.
- **Dying:** Guerrilla's respawn at a safehouse isn't ported yet. At the death screen, press **~** and then **R** to reload the last checkpoint. After that reload the people may be gone and vehicles from **F7 → Spawn** may not appear; for a full world, load a save or start the game again.
- **Story:** Guerrilla's missions and story come last of all.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from any earlier version:** your world has to be built again (the launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No). Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 3 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (a few minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu's SAVE GAME.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.5.10-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds the new settings to your file and keeps your values: `guerrilla_campaign=` under `[Mods]`, `DriverGunAsGuerrilla=` and `TurretHingesAsGuerrilla=` under `[GuerrillaVehicles]`, and a new `[GuerrillaCampaign]` section. Then build the world again, as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
