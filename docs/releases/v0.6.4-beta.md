A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.6.4-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.6.3-beta

**The level editor grows, thanks to Delgeth.**
- **New level** makes a Ruin or Infestation level, or a blank map: ground made from nothing (or none, for caves and interiors), at the size you choose, with the sky you choose. **Delete level** takes one away again.
- A **spawn resources** editor, and every object property can be edited.
- **Terrain:** sculpt the ground with hills, peaks, mountains, valleys, mesas, terraces, cliffs, canyons, dunes and craters, or place terrain pieces. Paint ground textures, roads and foliage, scatter chunks under the brush, or make new terrain. A zone's invisible walls can be picked, moved, added and deleted like objects.
- **Live editing:** with the game running, sculpted ground, chunks, level geometry, spawn nodes, pickups and turrets change in the game in place. **Game's level** opens the zone the game has loaded.
- Ruin can have more than five maps: add them to its map table.

**The editor shows the Guerrilla world's gameplay.** In a Guerrilla world zone, the level view now draws the places the ported gameplay uses, and the Scene panel lists them by kind under "Guerrilla's gameplay", each kind with an eye to hide it. Click one there for its details in the Inspector. They include activity, raid and mission starts, demolitions masters, safehouses, the people's action spots, kill regions, EDF restricted areas, Marauder ambush areas, kiosks and convoy starts. For now they can be looked at, not changed.

**Life in the towns.**
- People at Guerrilla's action spots walk to them and get on with it: Samanya welding, radio operators at the radio, VIPs with binoculars, clerks, people sitting and smoking. A fight breaks them off.
- People walking around stop at nearby spots to smoke, browse the shops, sit on benches, talk or look at a kiosk, then walk on. There are 1,839 such spots.
- Civilians no longer walk around with their rifles out. They draw a weapon when they join a fight.
- Guerrilla's grenade rule: the AI throws grenades only now and then, so a Marauder ambush can't knock you down until you die.

**Demolitions master.**
- The challenges that failed as soon as they started now work.
- A challenge gives you its own weapons and remote charges. You get your own back at the end, or at once if you die.
- Guerrilla's rule for running out of ammo, and the target building shown on the minimap.

**Also:**
- Guerrilla's walker at the Dust EDF base is visible and no longer throws you across the map.
- Change seat is on the middle mouse button, as the Keys window says. A seat binding an older build saved on the right button is moved.
- The weapon-swap prompt no longer overlaps the ammo you picked up, and Guerrilla's remote charge squares show the charges you have out.
- **F7 → Guerrilla → Keys** takes any key.
- Fixed: the game freezing when a vehicle's route went round in a loop, the "mission mempool" memory error while driving, and the "Failed pathfind" messages in Oasis and Parker.
- The vehicle cameras turn with the mouse at Guerrilla's speed.

Everything from v0.6.3-beta is here too: the districts' control and morale, respawning at safehouses, people joining your fights, Marauder ambushes, the story people, 64 cars, the kiosks and the minimap.

**Known problems:**
- **The game can still freeze now and then** (seen after falling out of the world, and once in a fight). If it does, wait a few seconds before closing it: the log then records where it was stuck. Please send `logs\rfa_mods.log`.
- **Guerrilla's walkers walk on the spot** when you drive one.
- **Kiosk broadcasts and other sounds don't fade with distance**: they play at full volume until the edge of their range, then stop.
- **Demolitions master:** the challenges' barrels and propane tanks aren't there yet.
- **Road signs** stop a car like a wall on the first hit, and only fall on the second.
- The smokers' cigarettes and Samanya's welding torch aren't there yet.
- In a narrow editor window, the level view's toolbar wraps onto two rows.
- Some of Guerrilla's materials and shaders are still Armageddon's; no music or NPC voices yet. Guerrilla's missions and story come last of all.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from any earlier version:** your world has to be built again (the launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No). Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 3 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (a few minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu: the Handbook and SAVE GAME are there.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.6.3-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds any new settings to your file and keeps your values. Then build the world again, as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
